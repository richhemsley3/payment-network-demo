One transaction, end to end


Five messages carry a payment from the terminal to the wire. Two are yours to send. Three arrive signed, at an endpoint you register once.



  
You sendPOST /acceptance/v1/authorizations
The issuer answers within 500 ms, or your stand-in rules do.

  
Arrivesauthorization.decisioned
Within a second of the answer, approved or declined.

  
You sendPOST /acceptance/v1/captures
Within 7 days. One call takes up to 5,000 ids.

  
Arrivessettlement.posted
When the cycle is final, with the wire reference.

  
Arrives if a cardholder disputesdispute.opened
The reason code and your deadline. Evidence goes to the Disputes endpoint.
