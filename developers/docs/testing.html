Declines

A decline is a 200 with decision: declined and the issuer's response code. A soft decline, 51, may be retried on your own schedule. A hard decline, 05 or 54, must not be retried with the same message. The codes say which is which.

Disputes

6011 3300 0000 2027 approves, then opens a dispute four minutes later: dispute.opened arrives with reason 4837 and a response deadline, and the case appears on the Disputes endpoint. Submit evidence to it and the sandbox decides within a minute.

Tokens and reissue

6011 3300 0000 3504 approves on a token whose card is reissued overnight. In the sandbox the reissue happens after two minutes: token.updated arrives and the token keeps working.

Sandbox and live

The base URL sets the environment: sandbox, certification, production. One set of credentials is promoted through them by the network; nothing on your side changes but the host. Live never takes test data, and a test card sent to production is declined with 14.
