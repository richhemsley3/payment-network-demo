Declines


A decline is a 200 with decision: declined and the issuer's response code. The codes say what each one means.



  
Soft, retry on your own schedule51 insufficient funds, 91 issuer unavailable. Not within 60 seconds.

  
Hard, ask for another card05 do not honor, 14 invalid number, 54 expired. The same message is never retried.




Disputes


6011 3300 0000 2027 approves, then opens a dispute four minutes later. The case appears on the Disputes endpoint, and the sandbox decides on your evidence within a minute.

Tokens and reissue


6011 3300 0000 3504 approves on a token whose card is reissued overnight. In the sandbox the reissue happens after two minutes, and the token keeps working.
