What certification is, what your host sends, what the network sends, and what activation does. The record of what the network saw is in the Developer Portal, after an agreement.

Before you startA signed participation agreement. Your configuration confirmed in the Developer Portal. A host that can reach cert.api.dgn.com over mutual TLS.

What certification is

A script your host produces and a record of what the network saw. The cases come from your configuration: the products you chose, the entry modes you accept, the stand-in rules you set. Typically 20 to 30. Nothing runs from the portal. Your host sends each case to the certification environment, and a case passes when the network sees it arrive with the expected answer.

Who sends what

Your host sendsAuthorizations on the test cards, reversals, evidence on a dispute, a token and a charge on it, the totals for a cycle, any request from your allowlisted range.
The network sendsOnly the cases that are the network's to send: event deliveries to your endpoint, the daily settlement file, a retrieval request, an arbitration decision, a token reissue.
A case passes whenThe network sees it, and what it saw matches the case: the response code, the amount, the delivery answered with a 2xx.

The case groups

Every case says what to send, what to expect, when the network saw it, and the result.

AuthorizationApprove card present and not present, partial, soft and hard decline, stand-in, reversal in full and in part. Your host sends all eight.
8 cases
ConnectivityMutual TLS on the production chain and the staging chain, which the network checks, and any request from your allowlisted range.
3 cases
DisputesA retrieval request and an arbitration decision the network delivers; evidence your host submits in time and, once, after the window.
4 cases
SettlementThe daily file and its adjustments, which the network sends; the totals for that cycle, which your host reports to the cent.
3 cases
TokensProvision a token and charge on it from your host; the network reissues the card and the token keeps working.
3 cases

Submit for validation

When every case is seen and passing, the Developer Portal offers one button, Submit for validation. Submission books the network's validation in your window: a Discover certification engineer validates end to end across every layer, and the result is written to the same record. Until then the page has no button; there is nothing to run.

What you upload

Staging certificate chainLeaf, intermediate, root. The network requires the full chain; most clients accept a short one, which is the usual first failure. The network checks the chain on request.
ConfigurationPre-filled from the agreement: BIN range, settlement account, dispute routing. You add the callback endpoint and the stand-in ceiling.
Production certificate chainBefore activation. Expiry is read on the first handshake and flagged at 30 days.
A production endpointProduction events go to a production URL with its own secret.

Activation

Scheduled by Discover, 6:00 AM ET on the agreed day. The same credentials open production; nothing on your side changes but the host. Production traffic appears in Logs from the first authorization, and the first settlement cycle posts the next morning.
